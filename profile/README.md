# NOBELMIND

**Where doctors get to feel like coders, and coders get to think like doctors.**

We are a health startup, bringing together a strong team of talented people to leverage AI for health. While doctors perform physical exams and operate, AI can maximize the value of medical therapy.

Physicians and engineers bring their specialization and ingenuity to build software that will expand the reach of medicine for everyone.

**[Between Visits](https://github.com/NOBELMIND/between-visits)**: an invented patient at home, two weeks of readings, and one question. You answer. Two weeks later we open the sealed answer and talk it through together.

> **Invented patients only. Not medical advice.**
> Please never post real patient information here, even de-identified. Edits stay visible in history.
